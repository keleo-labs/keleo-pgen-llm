# Focus map

Recipe version: 3.0.0. Read this before a team brief ties a person to a product,
partner group, quota or expertise. The map is `review.focus` and member
responsibilities in series.json. Do not read the retired skill-config section.

A focus fixture supplied for this brief replaces that section. Do not mix the fixture with the live map. If the user said not to use the focus map, or both keys are missing or `unset`, assign nobody. Do not invent a product, a partner group, or a quota figure.

## What a line means

Match the person by name or email in the configured team membership. People who
are not on the map get no product and no partner quota.

- A product line means that person's quota **is** the team's quota for that product. Say it that way. Do not describe it as a separate personal target.
- If the line says the quota covers two products, name both. Do not compare the amount to only one of those product lines in the business picture.
- A partner-group line is that group's quota. It is not a product quota or an aggregate product result.
- Where two lines cover the same product, say so. Do not add those quotas into one team total.

The amount still comes from the configured quota source or a fixture the user supplied. The map does not contain amounts. If sources label the same number differently, verify its scope before reporting it. If the source cannot be read, do not invent the figure. The role can still be stated.

A subject-matter line is a role. Name who leads and who else is an expert, including what they support. Do not turn that role into a quota, a forecast, or a bookings result.
