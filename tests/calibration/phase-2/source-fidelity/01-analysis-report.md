# Analysis Report — Platform Onboarding Field Notes

## 1. Scope

The source covers how delivery teams are onboarded onto an internal platform at one
organisation, drawn from the platform team's own field notes.

## 2. Concerns identified

**Onboarding progression.** A team moves from an access request, through a period of
accompanied work with an assigned platform engineer, to a first deployment. The source
describes the accompanied form only; it states the platform team's wish for onboarding
to happen without them, and records that they have not agreed what that looks like.

**Onboarding prerequisites.** Teams are expected to hold a source repository and to
have a named technical owner before onboarding begins. These expectations are not
published, and are enforced only by the assigned engineer remembering to ask.

**The written path.** No written onboarding path exists. Order of steps and the advice
given vary between platform engineers; two teams in one month received different advice
about resource limits.

**Friction records.** The platform team records the points where teams get stuck, in an
unstructured shared document.

## 3. Measurement

The source states that the platform team has **not** measured how long an onboarding
takes. No figures of any kind appear in the source.

## 4. Roles

One role is described: a platform engineer assigned to a team for the duration of its
onboarding. The source does not describe any role on the delivery team's side beyond
the "named technical owner" expected as a prerequisite.
