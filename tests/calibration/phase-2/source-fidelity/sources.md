# Source: Platform Onboarding Field Notes (2026)

These notes are the only source material for the calibration fixture. Anything a
downstream artifact asserts must trace back to something written here.

## How teams reach the platform

A delivery team's first contact with the platform is a request for access. Until
that request is granted, the team cannot see what the platform offers. Teams
report that the wait is the part they remember.

The platform team currently walks each new team through onboarding in person. One
platform engineer is assigned and stays with the team from the access request to
the team's first deployment. This works and does not scale: the platform team
spends most of a week per onboarding.

## What gets written down

There is no written onboarding path. Each platform engineer has their own order of
steps and their own set of things they remember to mention. Two teams onboarded in
the same month were given different advice about resource limits.

The platform team has started recording the points where a team got stuck. The list
is kept in a shared document and is not yet structured.

## Prerequisites

Teams are expected to have a source repository and a named technical owner before
onboarding starts. These expectations are not written down anywhere a delivery team
can find, and are enforced by the platform engineer remembering to ask.

## What the platform team wants

The platform team wants onboarding to happen without them. They have not agreed on
what that looks like in practice, and they have not measured how long an onboarding
currently takes.
