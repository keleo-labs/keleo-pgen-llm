## Evidence for group facilitation

Reference version: 1.0.1. These are possible design inputs from published
sources, not evidence that a technique has worked in this team. Locate the
sources through the configured reference collection and read relevant passages
before citing them. Do not cite lecture material as its underlying publication.

- Enberg, Lindkvist and Tell (2006), *Exploring the dynamics of knowledge integration*: the case examines interplay between individual work, meetings and ad hoc problem-solving. Use independent owner preparation followed by selective integration. A product-development case does not establish a universal meeting formula.
- Zaki (2025), *Every team needs a super-facilitator*: use complementary expertise, equitable contributions and trust to shape facilitation. Do not turn it into an assessment of personalities or assert a measured effect in this team.
- Frisch (2008), *When teams can't decide*: agree the outcome before comparing routes and broaden options where a choice is stuck. Use only when there is a real decision; disagreement is not proof of dysfunction.
- Marquet (2018), leadership conference transcript: invite owners to express intent within competence, clarity and authority. This is a speaker's account with automatic captions, not independent causal evidence; accepted local decision rights still govern.
